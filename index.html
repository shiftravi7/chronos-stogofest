II

The Science

The idea behind CHRONOS, in one picture.

Why Evening Screens Matter

Your body runs on a roughly 24-hour clock called the circadian rhythm. As evening falls it releases melatonin, the hormone that makes you feel sleepy. Bright screen light late at night can hold that signal back, so sleep comes later — but the alarm does not. In the teenage years the body clock also naturally shifts later, which makes late-night screens even harder on sleep.
